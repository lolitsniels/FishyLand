The Fishing Lands: a free fishing MMO in your browser

The Fishing Lands is a persistent multiplayer fishing game you play straight from your browser, on a computer or a phone, with nothing to download. Every catch is shared with real anglers in a living world: you see their trophies on the harbour board, trade with them on the market, join their crews and race them in tournaments.

About The Fishing Lands

The world is made of twelve waters, each with its own fish, mood and level requirement. You start at quiet Millbrook Pond and work your way through Willow River, Greyfell Lake, Saltmarsh Estuary, Harrow Point, Coral Shoals, Blackwater Mangroves, The Frozen Reach, The Sunken Deep and Ghostlight Cavern, all the way to The Drowned Temple and The Cinder Trench for the most experienced anglers.

Across those waters live 416 species: 144 common, 102 uncommon, 72 rare, 42 epic, 41 legendary and 15 mythic fish. Each one has its own size range, favourite bait and fighting style, and rare variants such as scarred, giant, albino and golden fish are worth far more than a normal catch. Your Journal records every species you land, and mastering a fish over many catches unlocks lasting bonuses.

Beyond fishing there is a full community game: a player-run market, crews with shared crew halls and crew bosses, weekly fishing duels, scheduled tournaments, daily quests, contracts from harbour patrons, achievements with pixel-art badges, a friends list and live chat.

Beginner's fishing guide and tutorial

1. Casting and the fight

Each cast uses a little energy, which refills over time. When a fish bites, a tension gauge appears. Hold the reel button (or the Space bar) to raise the line tension and let go to lower it. Keep the marker inside the green sweet spot to tire the fish out and fill the catch bar. Too much tension snaps the line, too little lets the fish run, and if the timer runs out the fish escapes.

2. Fish behaviour

Fish fight differently. Steady and sluggish fish are forgiving, runners pull hard and fast, thrashers shake the line, divers dive deep and jumpers leap. Bigger and rarer fish use tricks such as sudden dives, burst runs and late rallies, so watch the gauge closely as the fight goes on.

3. Choosing bait

  • Bread Crust, Maggots and Crickets: cheap baits that pond fish love.
  • Earthworms: the all-round freshwater bait.
  • Live Minnows, Spinner Lures and Cut Bait: for predators like pike, bass and zander.
  • Peeled Shrimp, Squid Strips and Bloodworms: for estuary, reef and sea fish.
  • Glow Jig and Premium Chum: for the deep and dark waters and for drawing in rare fish.

You can also craft your own bait from ingredients with Bait crafting.

4. Water types and progression

Early waters are calm and forgiving. Deeper waters get harder step by step: their rare fish pull harder and leave you less room for error, but every catch there pays extra gold and XP. Upgrade your rod, reel and line in the Tackle Shop before moving on, keep your gear repaired, and upgrade your favourite pieces at the Enhancement bench for extra power.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Fishing Lands free to play?

Yes. The whole game is free in your browser. You can start as a guest in one click and claim an account later to keep your progress.

How do I catch legendary and mythic fish?

Fish the water where they live (the Wiki and your Journal show where each species bites), use the bait they prefer, and bring gear strong enough for that water. Legendary and mythic fish appear rarely and fight hard, so a good reel and line make a big difference. Luck from better bait and the Lucky Tide booster raises your odds.

How do I earn double XP?

When a Harbour Lantern booster is lit, the whole server gets double XP, better vendor prices and faster energy refill. Daily quests, contracts, achievements and the daily login spin also give big amounts of XP.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The game is built for touch screens as well as desktops, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

How do I make gold?

Sell fish to the vendor, list catches and items on the player market, complete contracts and quests, and place well in tournaments and the weekly duel ladder.
