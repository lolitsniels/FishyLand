DESCRIPTION (paste into the description box)
---------------------------------------------
Grab a rod, pick your angler and come fish with us!

FishyLand is a free multiplayer fishing game you play right in your browser. Walk around a busy harbour, sail to 12 hand-painted waters and catch over 350 different fish, each with its own size, weight and rarity.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Catch 350+ fish: from tiny rudd to legendary sea monsters, every catch gets a card with its real length and weight
- Play with friends: see other anglers walking around, chat live and join a Crew with its own island and clubhouse
- Explore 12 waters: peaceful ponds, misty swamps, coral reefs, frozen lakes and the deep ocean, each with its own music
- Fight every bite: hold and release to keep the line tense, and watch out for fish with tricks of their own
- Upgrade your tackle: better rods, reels, lines and bait help you land bigger and rarer fish
- Fill your Fishdex: discover secret fish with riddles from the harbour folk and collect rare colour variants
- Duel other anglers: 3-minute live fishing duels at the Duel Pier

HOW TO PLAY
- Click or tap to walk, stand on a glowing ring and press FISH (or Space)
- Hold the mouse, finger or Space to reel, let go when the line gets too tight
- The Harbourmaster shows you everything in a short tutorial

Play as a guest in one click, or make a free account to keep your angler on any device. Your progress is the same on itch.io and on fishyland.com.

Made with love for anglers of all ages.
